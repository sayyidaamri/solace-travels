About Retreat

Come alone, leave with friends. This week in Ubud is made for solo travellers who want adventure, rest and good company. Jungle walks, temple visits, waterfalls and daily yoga fill the days, and the evenings are always shared.

Wayan and Kadek look after the group like family and make sure nobody ever feels left out.
Bali is green, spiritual and full of life. Ideal for anyone ready for a fresh start or simply a joyful week.
Start dates are available every week from January to May.

Join any week between January and May. The rainy months bring lush green
rice fields, warm showers in the afternoon and quiet temples.

Highlights include the Tegallalang rice terraces, a sunrise trek on Mount Batur,
the sacred spring of Tirta Empul and a day at a black sand beach on the east coast.

Team
