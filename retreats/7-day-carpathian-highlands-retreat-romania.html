About Retreat

Spend a quiet week in the Carpathian foothills, where snowy forests and old wooden villages set the pace. Mornings start with breathwork by the fire, afternoons are for slow hikes along shepherd trails, and evenings end with warm food and stories.

Ioana and Radu will shape the week around your energy, mixing gentle movement with long walks and time to simply rest.
The highlands are wild, calm and full of folklore. Ideal for anyone who needs fresh air, silence and a proper reset.
No experience is needed and every activity can be adapted to your level.

What Makes This Retreat Special

This is not a hotel stay with a schedule attached, it is a week of mountain life.
Our hosts open their home and their valley to you, and the rhythm of the place
does most of the work — you only need to show up and slow down.

Team

Radu Ionescu
Mountain Guide
Radu has walked these ridges since he was a boy and now leads small groups through them.

He is a certified mountain leader and wilderness first responder, and he knows every spring, hut and shortcut in the range.
Ioana Marin
Breathwork Coach
Ioana came back to her home village after a decade of city work and burnout.

She now teaches breathwork and gentle yoga, and believes the mountains are the best teacher she has ever had.
