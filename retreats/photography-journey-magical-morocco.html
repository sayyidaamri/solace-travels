About Retreat

Two weeks across Morocco with a camera in your hand and a guide at your side. From the blue lanes of Chefchaouen to the souks of Fes and the dunes of Merzouga, every day brings a new light to work with.

Youssef and Lena run small daily sessions on composition, street portraits and editing, and leave plenty of time to just look around.
Morocco is colourful, warm and generous with its stories. Ideal for anyone who wants to travel slowly and come home with real images.
Any camera works, including a phone, and beginners are very welcome.

What Makes This Retreat Special

We travel as a small group of curious people, not as tourists in a bus.
Our guides grew up in these streets, so you are welcomed into workshops,
kitchens and homes that most visitors never see.

camp near Merzouga and wander the old medina of Fes with a local storyteller.

Team

Youssef Amrani
Photographer & Guide
Youssef is a documentary photographer from Marrakesh with fifteen years of work for travel magazines.

He teaches with patience and humour, and is happiest in a busy market at six in the morning.
Lena Vogel
Editing Coach
Lena fell in love with Morocco on her first trip and has returned every year since.

She runs the editing sessions and helps each guest build a small portfolio from the journey.

Where we'll meet

Riad Dar Nour
Derb Sidi Bouloukat 23, Medina, Marrakesh 40000, Morocco
Our route takes in Marrakesh, the Tizi n'Tichka pass, Aït Benhaddou, the Dades and Todra gorges, the dunes of Erg Chebbi, Midelt and Fes. Expect long scenic drives broken up by tea stops, village walks and plenty of time for photographs.
