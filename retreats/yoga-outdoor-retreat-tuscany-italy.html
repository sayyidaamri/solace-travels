About Retreat

In the soft hills of the Val d'Orcia, take a few days to breathe with Giulia and Marco. Surrounded by olive groves and cypress lanes, this is a private or small group yoga retreat with optional guided walks and visits to medieval towns.

Giulia designs a yoga and food plan around your needs. She is a patient teacher who meets you where you are and helps you go a little further.
Tuscany is full of art, quiet landscapes and honest food. Ideal for anyone looking for stillness, beauty and good company.
All levels and ages are welcome and you can pick your own arrival date.

What Makes This Retreat Special

It is more than a yoga holiday — for a few days you become part of the house.
What matters most to us is to really meet each guest, to see what yoga means for
them, and to share the slow beauty of this land.

Team

Giulia Ferrante
Yoga Teacher
Giulia is a certified yoga teacher and she will lead this retreat.

Yoga has been part of her life since she was twenty, and she has studied Hatha and Vinyasa in Italy, Portugal and India.
Marco Bellini
Facilitator
Marco left a career in architecture to restore an old farmhouse and open it to guests.

He leads the walks, knows every trattoria in the valley and makes sure everyone feels at home from the first evening.
