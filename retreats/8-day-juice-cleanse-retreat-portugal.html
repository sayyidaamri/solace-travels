About Retreat

Reset your body by the Atlantic with a guided juice cleanse, ocean walks and daily yoga. The house sits above a quiet bay near Ericeira, with the sound of the waves in every room.

Ana prepares each day's juices and broths and checks in with you every morning, so the cleanse stays gentle and safe.
The Portuguese coast is wild, bright and full of fresh air. Ideal for anyone who wants to feel lighter and more rested.
A short call before arrival helps us adapt the plan to your health and goals.
