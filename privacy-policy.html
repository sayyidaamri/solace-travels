Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected
Personal Data
While using our Service, We may ask You for certain information that can be used to
contact or identify You. This may include, but is not limited to:
  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Phone number
  • Travel preferences You choose to share
Usage Data
Usage Data is collected automatically when You use the Service.
It may include Your Device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages You visit,
the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages and other diagnostic data.

When You access the Service from a mobile device, We may also collect the type of device,
its operating system and the type of mobile browser You use.
Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar technologies to remember Your preferences and understand
how the Service is used. You can tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to warn You
when one is sent; if You do, some parts of the Service may not work as intended.
  • Necessary Cookies keep the Service working, for example by remembering Your
    cookie choice.
  • Preference Cookies remember choices such as language or region.
  • Analytics Cookies help Us understand which pages are visited most.
Use of Your Personal Data
We may use Personal Data for the following purposes:
  • To provide and maintain the Service and to answer Your requests.
  • To manage bookings and send You information about Your retreat.
  • To contact You by email or phone about updates or changes.
  • To improve the Service, our retreats and Your experience.

Disclosure of Your Personal Data

Retreat Partners
To organise Your trip, We share the details that are strictly needed with the hosts,
coaches and accommodation partners of the retreat You book.
Law Enforcement
In some cases We may be required to disclose Personal Data by law or in response
to valid requests from public authorities.
Security of Your Personal Data
We care about the security of Your Personal Data, but no method of transmission over
the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We use commercially reasonable
means to protect it, yet We cannot guarantee absolute security.
